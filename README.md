- **Why I am recording this for you:** I find it really hard to explain myself face-to-face without getting overwhelmed or losing my temper, and I don't know if you would listen to me patiently. In 10th grade, I learned a quote by Anne Frank that really stuck with me: _"Paper has more patience than people."_ That is why I always write my thoughts down in my notes, and why I recorded this message—so I can be completely real with you and you can hear what is actually going on in my mind.
    
- **Why I want to switch to a "dummy school" for 12th grade:** Going to regular school for 10 hours a day completely drains my energy. Nobody actually studies there, and the other kids tease me and talk nonsense to me all day long. If I complain to the teachers, the kids only tease me more, so I have to either pretend to be like them or sit quietly in one place all day just to survive it. When I was in section AJ2, I barely even left my seat except to use the washroom once a day. For 12th grade, please admit me into the dummy school in Koti where attendance isn't required. I just want to stay in my room, study 17 to 18 hours a day on my own, and go for evening walks to keep my mind fresh.
    
- **My 10th board results prove that I do better studying at home:** If you doubt whether I can study on my own, just look at my 10th-grade board exams. Because I took two months off and studied at home, I scored **88.4%**—beating both Shubhankar (85.4%) and Ayushi (85.3%)—and missed full marks in Social Studies by only two points. In my recent 11th-grade exams, the only reason I failed two subjects and got 19 out of 60 in Chemistry was because my handwriting and presentation were terrible, not because I didn't study.
    
- **How I actually use my laptop and the internet:** You think that simply turning the internet on or off solves everything, without ever looking patiently at what I am doing. Check my browser history—I don't touch YouTube at all. In fact, I installed Linux and made my screen completely gray and minimal just to stay away from distractions. When I use the internet, I use Claude (an AI tool) to read explanations word-for-word—70% for my studies and 30% for general curiosity. I also write notes in Obsidian, download website articles so I can read them offline without internet, and plan to study from Physics Wallah (PW) online batches. Other kids like Ahan have their own SIM card with 2 GB of data to clear their doubts anytime at home, whereas I sit stuck for an hour waiting for internet on my laptop, which only builds up my frustration and wastes my time.
    
- **How I have been feeling and why I lose my temper:** Honestly, I haven't laughed or felt genuinely happy in the last four to six months. It hurts when you make jokes at my expense, compare me to other kids without realizing every child is different, or speak to me in a fake, overly polite tone like strangers instead of my parents. I know I sometimes lose control of my anger—like when I threw the already-broken mouse earlier—but I read that at my age, emotions develop much faster than the frontal cortex of the brain, which controls impulses and decision-making. I am not trying to be your enemy; I am just under a lot of stress about my future, my curiosity is dying under all these rigid stereotypes, and I need you to trust me to work hard in my own way.

ବିଷୟରେ ଫେଲ୍ ହେଲି ଏବଂ କେମିଷ୍ଟ୍ରିରେ ୬୦ ରୁ ୧୯ ରଖିଲି, ତାର ଏକମାତ୍ର କାରଣ ଥିଲା ମୋର ଖରାପ ହ୍ୟାଣ୍ଡରାଇଟିଂ (ହସ୍ତାକ୍ଷର) ଓ ପ୍ରେଜେଣ୍ଟେସନ୍, ପାଠ ନ ପଢ଼ିବା ନୁହେଁ।ମୁଁ ପ୍ରକୃତରେ ମୋ ଲ୍ୟାପଟପ୍ ଏବଂ ଇଣ୍ଟରନେଟ୍ କିପରି ବ୍ୟବହାର କରେ: ଆପଣ ଭାବୁଛନ୍ତି ଯେ କେବଳ ଇଣ୍ଟରନେଟ୍ ଦେଇଦେଲେ ବା ବନ୍ଦ କରିଦେଲେ ସବୁ କାମ ସରିଗଲା, କିନ୍ତୁ ମୁଁ ସେଥିରେ କ’ଣ କରୁଛି ତାହା ଆପଣ କେବେ ଧୈର୍ଯ୍ୟର ସହ ଦେখନ୍ତି ନାହିଁ। ମୋର ବ୍ରାଉଜର୍ ହିଷ୍ଟ୍ରି ଚେକ୍ କରି ଦେଖନ୍ତୁ—ମୁଁ ୟୁଟ୍ୟୁବ୍ (YouTube) ବିଲକୁଲ୍ ଛୁଏଁ ନାହିଁ। ବରଂ, ଧ୍ୟାନ ଭଙ୍ଗ କରୁଥିବା ଜିନିଷରୁ ଦୂରେଇ ରହିବା ପାଇଁ ମୁଁ ଲିനକ୍ସ (Linux) ଇନଷ୍ଟଲ୍ କରିଛି ଏବଂ ସ୍କ୍ରିନକୁ ପୂରା ଗ୍ରେ (ଧୂସର) ଓ ସିମ୍ପଲ୍ କରିଦେଇଛି। ଯେତେବେଳେ ମୁଁ ଇଣ୍ଟରନେଟ୍ ବ୍ୟବହାର କରେ, ମୁଁ କ୍ଲଡ୍ (Claude - ଏକ AI ଟୁଲ୍) ବ୍ୟବହାର କରି ଗୋଟିଏ ଗୋଟିଏ ଶବ୍ଦ ପଢ଼େ—୭୦% ମୋ ପାଠପଢ଼ା ପାଇଁ ଏବଂ ୩୦% ଅନ୍ୟାନ୍ୟ ବିଷୟରେ ଜାଣିବା ପାଇଁ। ଏହା ଛଡ଼ା ମୁଁ ଅବସିଡିଆନ୍ (Obsidian) ରେ ନୋଟ୍ସ ଲେଖେ, ୱେବସାଇটରୁ ଆର୍ଟିକିଲ୍ ଡାଉନଲୋଡ୍ କରି ରଖେ ଯାହାଫଳରେ ବିନା ଇଣ୍ଟରନେଟରେ ଅଫଲାଇନ୍ ପଢ଼ିପାରିବି, ଏବଂ ଫିଜିକ୍ସ ୱାଲା (PW) ର ଅନଲାଇନ୍ ବ୍ୟାଚ୍ ରୁ ପଢ଼ିବାକୁ ଯୋଜନା କରୁଛି। ଆହାନ ଭଳି ଅନ୍ୟ ପିଲାମାନଙ୍କ ପାଖରେ ଘରେ ବସି ଡାଉଟ୍ କ୍ଲିଅର୍ କରିବା ପାଇଁ 2 GB ଡାଟାର ନିଜସ୍ୱ ସିମ୍ କାର୍ଡ ଅଛି, କିନ୍ତୁ ମତେ ମୋ ଲ୍ୟାପଟপରେ ଇଣ୍ଟରନେଟ୍ ପାଇଁ ଘଣ୍ଟା ଘଣ୍ଟା ଅପେକ୍ଷା କରି ବସିବାକୁ ପଡ଼ୁଛି, ଯାହା କେବଳ ମୋର ରାଗ ବଢ଼ାଉଛି ଏବଂ ସମୟ ନଷ୍ଟ କରୁଛି।ମୁଁ କିପରି ଅନୁଭବ କରୁଛି ଏବଂ ମତେ କାହିଁକି ରାଗ ଆସୁଛି: ସତ କହିବାକୁ ଗଲେ, ଗତ ଚାରି ରୁ ଛଅ ମାସ ହେଲାଣି ମୁଁ ମନ ଖୋଲି ହସିନାହିଁ କିମ୍ବା ଖୁସି ଅନୁଭବ କରିନାହିଁ। ମତେ ବହୁତ ଖରାପ ଲାଗେ ଯେତେବେଳେ ଆପଣ ମୋ ଉପରେ ମଜାକ କରନ୍ତି, ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ ପିଲା ଅଲଗା ବୋଲି ନ ବୁଝି ଅନ୍ୟ ପିଲାଙ୍କ ସହ ମୋର ତୁଳନା କରନ୍ତି, କିମ୍ବା ବାପା-ମା’ଙ୍କ ଭଳି କଥା ନ ହୋଇ ଅଚିହ୍ନା ଲୋକଙ୍କ ଭଳି ଗୋଟିଏ ମିଛ ବା ତିଆରିଆ ସ୍ୱରରେ (fake tone) କଥା ହୁଅନ୍ତି। ମୁଁ ଜାଣେ ଯେ ବେଳେବେଳେ ମୁଁ ମୋ ରାଗ ଉପରେ ନିୟନ୍ତ୍ରଣ ରଖିପାରେ ନାହିଁ—ଯେମିତି ଆଗରୁ ଭାଙ୍ଗିଯାଇଥିବା ମାଉସଟିକୁ ମୁଁ ଫୋପାଡ଼ି ଦେଇଥିଲି—କିନ୍ତୁ ମୁଁ ପଢ଼ିଛି ଯେ ମୋ ବୟସରେ ମସ୍ତିଷ୍କର 'ଫ୍ରଣ୍ଟାଲ୍ କର୍ଟେକ୍ସ' (ଯାହା ନିଷ୍ପତ୍ତି ନେବା ଏବଂ ରାଗ ନିୟନ୍ତ୍ରଣ କରିବା କାମ କରେ) ତୁଳନାରେ ଭାବନାଗୁଡ଼ିକ (emotions) ବହୁତ ଶୀଘ୍ର ବଢ଼ିଥାଏ। ମୁଁ ଆପଣଙ୍କର ଶତ୍ରୁ ହେବାକୁ ଚାହୁଁନାହିଁ; ମୁଁ କେବଳ ମୋ ଭବିଷ୍ୟତକୁ ନେଇ ବହୁତ ଚିନ୍ତାରେ (ଟେନସନରେ) ଅଛି, ଏହି ପୁରୁଣା ଚିନ୍ତାଧାରା (stereotypes) ଭିତରେ ମୋର ଜାଣିବାର ଆଗ୍ରହ (curiosity) ମରିଯାଉଛି, ଏବଂ ମୁଁ ଚାହୁଁଛି ଆପଣ ମୋ ଉପରେ ବିଶ୍ୱାସ ରଖନ୍ତୁ ଯାହାଫଳରେ ମୁଁ ମୋ ନିଜ ବାଟରେ କଠିନ ପରିଶ୍ରମ କରିପାରିବି।
